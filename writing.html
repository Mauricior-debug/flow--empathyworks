Executive education

Embracing Progress

Next Steps for the Future of Work. This is an Executive MBA program textbook and required reading for several management-school leadership courses.

It is the book for leaders who need a structured view of what comes next in work: more digital, more decentralized, and less forgiving of purely transactional management.

Available from Amazon.

For managers on the job

Empathy Works

The Key to Competitive Advantage in the New Era of Work. Sophie shows why empathy is a corporate value, a mindset, and a skill for engagement, productivity, and growth.

Readers learn to cultivate empathy through the customer journey and the employee journey: culture and leadership, distributed workers, sales teams, and generations. The practice is empathy’s three steps, turned into habits with prospects, customers, and teammates.

  • A human-centric system for faster, less predictable work
  • Why empathy matters for sustainable growth
  • New habits for daily interactions

Cracking the Code of Behavioral Change for Employees’ Healthy (and More Productive) Well-being — a conversation with Dr. Rajiv Kumar, President and Chief Medical Officer of Virgin Pulse, on why people stick with routines and how behavioral science helps them change.
