Workshops

A workshop is where the habit starts.

The future of work is already digitized, distributed, and dynamic. Sophie’s workshops use exercises, not only slides, so managers and teams leave with a routine they can keep.

Plan a workshop See the three workshops

What people practice

  • Sales teams using empathy to increase conversions
  • Leaders motivating and supporting employees
  • Intergenerational teams having productive conversations
  • Managers and remote workers collaborating

How a session is built

Sessions are customized for executives, managers, and teams, in person or virtual. Sophie involves the whole room. She uses a long-tail approach: people leave with plans for small behavior changes that can become lasting routines.

Experiencing the new routine in the room is what makes adoption more likely afterward.

Workshop 01

Empathy at work

Integrating empathy into work practices and environments. Two versions: selling, and leading.

Selling with empathy

Empathy is how salespeople understand what a prospect is going through and stay useful to a customer they already have. This version shows sales leaders how to put empathy into management and training, and helps reps deepen relationships.

  • How empathy affects individual and team sales results
  • The components to practice: think, feel, and act
  • Connecting with a prospect’s situation and needs
  • Targeting and explaining a relevant solution
  • How a deeper customer relationship supports upselling
  • Conditions for an open, empathetic organization
  • Exercises that launch a habit and keep it going

Leading with empathy

For leaders who need trust, support, and teamwork while business conditions stay unpredictable. Participants look at where an empathetic approach changes a leadership moment, and they practice a response that fits what that person is actually dealing with.

  • Why empathy matters in a leadership role
  • How empathy works: think, feel, and act
  • Leadership moments that shift when the approach is empathetic
  • Reading different points of view and reactions
  • Adapting guidance so it fits the person
  • Habits with results you can notice

Exercises use situations, stories, and short games so people try a different response before they are back in the real meeting. Plan this workshop

Workshop 02

Integrating generations

Productive interactions across Boomers, Gen X, Millennials, and Gen Z.

Built for the whole workforce — executives, managers, and teams — when age groups misread each other, or when you want the range of perspectives actually used in the work.

  • Share personal context and find common ground
  • Understand coworkers’ mindsets well enough to improve the interaction
  • Catch a miscommunication by recognizing another point of view
  • See how more perspectives change an outcome
  • Outline questions that check whether the project is actually understood
  • Frame issues in terms people can share
  • Build cross-generational relationships that help collaboration

Pairs and groups replay real scenarios, practice deep listening, and leave with micro-steps for a routine that can last. Plan this workshop

Workshop 03

Working everywhere

High-performing teams that are not in one place.

For dispersed teams, including employees and the freelancers and contractors who now sit inside the work. People map how, when, and where they do their best work, then adjust the team setup so those preferences can coexist.

  • Why a colleague’s best work setting matters
  • Personal work styles, profiles, and priority performance factors
  • Oversight preferences and the technical support people need
  • Empathy skills for distributed interactions
  • Habits for working with people in other locations
  • Connecting with a co-worker’s experience so collaboration improves

Relationship is treated as the base layer. Sophie adds activities the team can continue after the session. Plan this workshop

Tell Sophie who will be in the workshop.

Sales, leaders, a mixed-age team, or people spread across locations. She will match the session and the amount of practice to that group.
