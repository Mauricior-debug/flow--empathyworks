Four ways to work with Sophie

The right next step depends on whether you are booking a room, changing a team’s habits, advising an executive group, or building the skill yourself.

  1. 01 Book a speaker Keynotes and interactive sessions for conferences, offsites, and user events. Sophie designs the hour around the result you need. See speaking
  2. 02 Bring a workshop Sales teams, leaders, generations, and people working across locations practice new habits instead of only hearing about them. See workshops
  3. 03 Advise the organization Flexcel Network works with executive teams on talent priorities and the operating changes that keep a distributed workforce effective. See consulting
  4. 04 Learn the practice LinkedIn Learning courses, the Empathy Works learning center, and two books for people who need the skill on the job. See courses

The work

Three practices, used together

The same themes show up in her speaking, workshops, consulting, and writing. Empathy is the skill that connects them.

Empathy at work

Empathy as a value, a mindset, and a skill with three moves: think, feel, and act. Used in leadership, sales, and daily routines.

Integrating generations
